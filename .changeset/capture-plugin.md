Add the opt-in `capture()` plugin: a camera that copies a screenshot of the page (or the device Frame's page) without the toolbar, a crop button to drag out a region to copy, More tools items to save a screenshot or a full-page one, and a record button that saves a screen recording through the browser's screen sharing. Screenshots are rendered with modern-screenshot, loaded on first use.
